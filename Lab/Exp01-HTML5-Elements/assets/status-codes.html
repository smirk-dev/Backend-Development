Codes I keep forgetting

201
Created — the row actually landed in the table.
204
No content — it worked, there is just nothing to send back.
302
Found — temporary redirect, browser will come back here later.
409
Conflict — usually a duplicate email in my case.
422
Unprocessable — the body parsed, the validation did not.
502
Bad gateway — my server died, nginx is being polite about it.
